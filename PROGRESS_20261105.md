## Parameter ranges

- 8 astrophysical parameters: identical to phase53/BTemu ranges
  (`F_STAR10 [-3,0]`, `ALPHA_STAR [-0.5,1]`, `F_ESC10 [-3,0]`, `ALPHA_ESC [-1,0.5]`, `M_TURN [8,10]`, `t_STAR [0.01,1]`, `L_X [38,42]`, `NU_X_THRESH [100,1500]`)
- KP/MS: present in BTemu (model side), **absent** in the phase59 PS emulator (fixed 1.0/1.5) — this is why the 10-D PS dataset is being built
- MS < 1.5 produces zero-signal runs (BT model invalid); those design points are filtered out
